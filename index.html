FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a U.S. tourist visa cost?

Our service fee depends on the complexity of your case, the number of applicants and the agreed scope of support. Our fees are charged for private advisory and preparation services only and are separate from official visa fees payable directly to the relevant U.S. authorities.

Official U.S. visa application information: travel.state.gov ↗

Can I apply after a previous refusal?

Yes. A previous refusal does not automatically prevent you from applying again. Before reapplying, it is important to understand your previous application and what has changed.

Can I apply from another country?

In general, you may apply in your country of nationality or in a country where you are legally resident. The right process depends on your circumstances and the current rules of the relevant U.S. embassy.

Should I buy flight tickets before getting my visa?

No. We recommend waiting until your visa has been issued before making non-refundable travel arrangements.

Do I need to attend an interview?

For most first-time applicants, an in-person interview is required. Some applicants may qualify for an interview waiver when renewing a visa.

How long does the process take?

The timeline depends on consular appointment wait times in the city where you apply and individual processing by U.S. authorities. During our consultation, we explain how official appointment scheduling works and the options available around your travel dates. Earlier appointments cannot be guaranteed.

Can I get a U.S. visa without previous international travel?

Yes. Limited travel history does not automatically prevent you from obtaining a visa. Each application is assessed individually.

Can you guarantee that I will get a visa?

No one can guarantee a U.S. visa — the final decision is made by a U.S. consular officer. Our role is to help you prepare a complete, accurate application and to prepare you for the interview.
